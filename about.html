The target of Guide The Damascus Events Guide aims to provide citizens and visitors with a reliable platform that showcases cultural, artistic, social, and entertainment events taking place in the city. It helps users discover events easily, stay informed about upcoming activities, and engage with the vibrant life of Damascus.
The Vision of Guide Our vision is to become the leading digital reference for events in Damascus, promoting cultural diversity, encouraging community participation, and highlighting the city’s rich heritage through well-organized and accessible event information.
The Message To Society We believe that events bring people together and strengthen social bonds. Through this guide, we seek to support local initiatives, celebrate creativity, and inspire hope and unity by connecting society with meaningful cultural and recreational experiences.

The Policies of Initialising Event

  • All events must align with the cultural, social, and ethical values of Damascus and respect public traditions and diversity.
  • Event information should be accurate, clear, and complete, including date, location, and type of activity.
  • Events should aim to promote community engagement, creativity, and positive social interaction.
  • Any event that incites violence, discrimination, or hatred will not be accepted or published.

The Rules of Publishing

  • Event organizers must provide valid and up-to-date details before publishing any event.
  • Submitted content should be original and must not violate intellectual property rights.
  • Images and media used in the event must be appropriate and relevant to the event itself.
  • The website administration reserves the right to review, edit, or reject any event submission that does not comply with publishing rules.
